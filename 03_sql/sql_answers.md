SQL Business Analysis - QuickPay Case Study
Project Overview
This document provides a detailed analysis of the transaction data processed by QuickPay. All queries were executed on the cleaned_transactions table to ensure the highest level of accuracy and consistency in the results.

Q1: Transaction Volume by Status
Objective: To determine the total count of transactions for every status type.

Approach: The data is grouped by the status_clean column to provide a high-level count of captured, failed, and chargeback transactions.

Business Value: This metric helps management monitor the overall health of the payment platform and identify any sudden increases in failed transactions.

Q2: Merchant Performance (Captured GMV)
Objective: To calculate the total Gross Merchant Volume (GMV) in USD for successful payments.

Approach: We filtered the dataset for 'captured' transactions and summed the amount_usd for each merchant.

Business Value: This allows the finance department to track actual revenue generated by each merchant partner.

Q3: Top 10 Merchants by Revenue
Objective: To identify the top 10 merchants contributing the highest revenue.

Approach: Using the captured volume logic from Q2, we sorted the merchants in descending order and limited the results to the top 10.

Business Value: This helps the sales team prioritize relationships with high-volume merchants.

Q4: Daily Business Trends
Objective: To monitor the daily growth of transaction volume and success counts.

Approach: Data is grouped by date to calculate the daily sum of USD amounts and the count of successful transactions.

Business Value: Daily tracking helps in identifying seasonal trends or specific days where technical issues may have occurred.

Q5: High-Risk Merchants (Chargeback Ratio)
Objective: To find merchants whose chargeback rate exceeds 1%.

Approach: We calculated the ratio of chargebacks to total transactions. Merchants with a ratio higher than 0.01 (1%) are filtered into this list.

Business Value: High chargeback rates are a major risk. Identifying these merchants helps prevent potential fines from card networks like Visa or Mastercard.

Q6: Geographic Risk Analysis
Objective: To identify regions with high average risk scores and significant transaction volume.

Approach: We analyzed regions with more than 20 transactions and an average risk score greater than 50.

Business Value: This allows the operations team to apply stricter security protocols in specific high-risk geographic areas.

Q7: Fraud Detection (Frequent Failures)
Objective: To detect users who experience multiple payment failures in a single day.

Approach: The query flags any user_id who has 3 or more failed or chargeback transactions within the same 24-hour period.

Business Value: This is a key fraud prevention tool to stop "card testing" where a malicious actor tries many stolen cards quickly.

Q8: Merchant Risk Scorecard
Objective: To provide a comprehensive summary of risk metrics per merchant.

Approach: We combined the count of chargebacks, the number of unique users impacted, and the total financial loss into a single report.

Business Value: This serves as a primary dashboard for the risk management team to evaluate the safety profile of every merchant.
